FEATURES AND BENEFITS

Ape-style Handlebars

Mid-ape handlebars offer riders a characterful stance with an upright riding posture, while simultaneously reducing strain on the upper body and making even long rides comfortable.

Low-slung Seat

The low-slung, bobber-style seat ensures relaxed ergonomics and enhanced comfort, whether you’re going for a short spin or a long trip.

Edge-laced Alloy Spoke Wheels and Whitewall Tubeless Tyres

The Goan Classic 350’s edge-laced aluminium spoke wheels and retro-styled tubeless tyres offer a dash of old-school, while reducing the risk of punctures and ensuring easy repairs.

All-LED Lighting

The signature Classic casquette headlamp returns with an LED upgrade, alongside LED turn indicators, offering powerful illumination and enhanced efficiency.

Retro-Analogue Dashboard

Get all the fun of a retro speedometer with the modern functionality of our Tripper Navigation System and a digital LCD display that lets you track everything from gear position to fuel levels and more.

Slip - Assist Clutch

Lighter Clutch Action and slicker gearshifts to keep you nimble and responsive. 

Type C USB with Fast Charging 

Stay plugged in even when you ride out, thanks to the Type-C fast charging port.

    11. New LED tail lamp
    12. Edge-laced Alloy Tubeless Spoke Wheels
    13. Bobber-inspired Rider Seat

    The new Royal Enfield Goan Classic 350 retains the proven J-series, long-stroke 349cc air-cooled engine which produces 20.2 BHP @ 6100 rpm and peak torque of 27 Nm @ 4000 rpm with many modern features.

    The default seat configuration for new Royal Enfield Goan Classic 350 is a dual seat, which can be easily removed. The pillion seat features a quick, bolt-on design for switching between single and dual seat configurations

    The new Royal Enfield Goan Classic 350 not only retains the quintessential features of the Classic 350 but also creates its niche proposition with standout features like raised handle bars, lower seat, whitewall tyres, vibrant CTGs & relaxed riding stance. It gives a more personalized touch to the riders who live by the values imbibed in the bobber DNA.

    The ape style handlebars add to the overall aesthetics of the Goan Classic 350. However, a Classic touring handlebars is also available as part of the GMA as an alternative for those who prefer a traditional setup.
