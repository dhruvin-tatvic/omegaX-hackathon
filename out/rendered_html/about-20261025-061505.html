Our Story

Born on Indian Roads,
Built for Every Road.

Apex Moto was born from a simple belief — that Indian riders deserved motorcycles that matched their passion, spirit, and ambition.

Our Mission

Redefining the Joy of Riding

At Apex Moto, we don't just build motorcycles — we build experiences. Each machine in our lineup is the result of thousands of hours of engineering, testing on real Indian roads, and listening to the riders who trust us with their journey.

We believe that a motorcycle is more than transport. It's freedom. It's character. It's the clearest expression of who you are and where you're going.

Milestones That Define Us

2014

Founded
