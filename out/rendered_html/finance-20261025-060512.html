How Is Your EMI Calculated?

Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) is calculated using the standard reducing balance loan formula:

EMI = [P × R × (1 + R)^N] / [(1 + R)^N - 1]
  • P (Principal Loan Amount): Ex-showroom price minus your down payment.
  • R (Monthly Interest Rate): Annual interest rate divided by 12 and then by 100.
  • N (Loan Tenure): Loan duration in total number of months (typically 12 to 72 months).

* On-road costs (RTO registration and insurance) can also be bundled into the loan with select banking partners.
